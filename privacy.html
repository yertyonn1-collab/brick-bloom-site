Privacy policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026
Developer: Abdulrauf
Contact: brickbloomsupport@gmail.com

This policy describes information used by Brick Bloom, Android package com.brickbloom.game.

Progress on your device

The game stores settings, scores, earned coins and stars, unlocked cosmetics, favourite worlds and saved runs in local app storage. Core play does not require a Brick Bloom account. This build disables Android cloud backup for its game data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes local progress.

Advertising and consent

Brick Bloom uses Google AdMob to show ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to handle applicable advertising choices. The advertising SDK can process IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. These third-party transmissions use encrypted connections.

Available consent choices depend on your region and account configuration. Where offered, use Settings → Privacy choices in the game. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent ordinary free retries. Ad availability depends on connectivity and providers.

Optional online scores

When Google Play Games rankings are enabled, the game uses Google's authentication service. Choosing Submit best & view leaderboard submits your eligible Time Attack score to Google Play Games. Your Play Games profile and score may be visible according to your Google profile settings. The game does not ask for your Google password or operate a separate developer-hosted player account database. You can play offline without submitting a score. Manage or delete game history through Google Play Games account settings.

Sharing

You may choose to share a challenge code or score image through Android's share menu. The selected receiving app handles that content under its own policy. Brick Bloom does not automatically post it. There is no public chat or community upload feed in this release.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall. We do not operate a cloud save service for this data. Google retains advertising and Play Games information under its own policies; use Google's account and advertising controls for that information. Contact brickbloomsupport@gmail.com for privacy questions. If you contact support, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are processed through Gmail to respond to your request. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us; any information that must be retained for legal reasons is excluded from deletion for that period. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Children

Brick Bloom is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults, and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate. Advertising consent requirements may vary by age and location.

Security and your choices

Local progress stays in app storage; third-party online services use encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Contact us about data held through support. Use Google's controls for information managed by Google.

This support website

This static website does not use our own analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or account system. This website is hosted using GitHub Pages when accessed through its published website address. GitHub processes visitor IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement. Email links open your chosen email application; nothing is sent until you send the message.
