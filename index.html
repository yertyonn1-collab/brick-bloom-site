Game support

Email brickbloomsupport@gmail.com with your phone model, Android version, game version and a short description of the issue. A world code or screenshot can help us reproduce it.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or Google sign-in codes.

Your progress

Game progress is stored on your device. Before troubleshooting, avoid uninstalling the game or clearing its storage, as these actions remove local progress.

Privacy questions and support-email deletion requests can be sent to the same support address.
